# Rules reference

The intended reference is the **original Cepheus Engine SRD**. The exact edition,
authoritative source, relevant sections, and applicable licensing terms have not
yet been verified. Compatibility is a goal, not a claim of affiliation or certification.

## Mechanics to verify

- Jump fuel consumption, including whether shorter jumps consume fuel by actual
  distance or ship rating.
- Tank capacity, other fuel reserves, and fuel types.
- Fuel prices and availability; scooping eligibility and processing requirements.
  Having fuel scoops alone does not settle these mechanics.
- Travel and refueling time, and any risks that affect the selected model.

Record source sections and the chosen interpretation here as mechanics are
implemented. The existing hex-distance utility is grid geometry, not an
implementation of jump or fuel rules.

## Simplifications

The fixed scenario in [product.md](product.md) bounds the initial model; it is not a
verified ship design. No fuel model or additional rules simplifications have been
adopted yet. Record deliberate departures from the reference explicitly.

## Content and licensing

Before including rules-derived content, verify the SRD's license, Open Game Content
designation, required notices, and attribution. Compatibility wording and trademark
treatment also remain unverified.

Summarize only the mechanics needed by the application and cite their sources.
Do not reproduce unnecessary SRD text or import proprietary Traveller text, maps,
setting data, artwork, or logos. Keep authored fictional data separate from rules
provenance. No rules interpretation or licensing determination is established here.
